What I Believe

People first.
AI second.

These are the rules I run my own business by. If they don't sit right with you, we're probably not a great fit. And that's ok.

  1. 01

    AI should make your team better, not smaller.

    I use AI to give the people you already have more time and headspace for the work they're good at. Not to replace them.

  2. 02

    Treat AI like a new team member.

    Brief it clearly, check its work and keep a person in charge. It's a tool you manage, not something you hand the keys to.

  3. 03

    Good results still need a human.

    AI on its own looks like AI. It takes business sense, design sense and an understanding of people to make it feel real.

  4. 04

    You should own it.

    I teach you how everything works, so you're never locked in to me or to any one tool.
